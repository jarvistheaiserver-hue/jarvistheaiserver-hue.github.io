Jarvis AI Assistant - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Jarvis is a privately operated personal AI assistant, not a publicly available commercial service.

Information Accessed

Jarvis may access Google Drive files, spreadsheets, and other Google account information that its authorized users explicitly permit through Google OAuth.

How Information Is Used

Authorized Google data is used to perform user-requested tasks, including document organization, travel planning, and personal productivity.

Storage and Security

Jarvis uses a privately managed server. OAuth credentials are stored privately and are not published on this website. Access is restricted to authorized users.

Data Sharing

Jarvis does not sell Google user data. Data may be processed by configured third-party services when required for an authorized task, subject to their respective privacy practices.

Retention and Deletion

Authorized users can revoke Google access through their Google Account security settings. Stored data and credentials can be deleted by the system operator upon request.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the Jarvis system operator through the account used to authorize Jarvis.
