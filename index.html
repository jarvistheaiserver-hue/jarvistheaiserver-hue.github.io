# Jarvis AI Assistant **A private, personal AI assistant** Jarvis is a privately operated AI assistant designed to help its authorized users manage personal information, organize documents, plan travel, and automate everyday tasks. ## Google Account Integration Jarvis uses Google OAuth to access Google services only when authorized by the account owner. Depending on the permissions granted, Jarvis may access Google Drive files, spreadsheets, and other supported Google services to perform user-requested tasks. Jarvis is not a commercial product and is not available for public registration. ## Privacy Read our [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html) for information about how Google account data is handled.
