Private Personal AI Assistant

About Jarvis

Jarvis is a privately operated AI assistant designed to help authorized users organize information, plan travel, manage documents, and automate everyday tasks.

Jarvis is not a publicly available commercial application.

Google Integration

Jarvis connects to Google services through OAuth authorization. Depending on the permissions granted, it may access Google Drive files, spreadsheets, and other authorized Google services to carry out user-requested tasks.

Google account access can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings.

Privacy and Security

Jarvis operates on a privately managed server. Access to its services is restricted to authorized users.

For more information, read the Jarvis Privacy Policy.
